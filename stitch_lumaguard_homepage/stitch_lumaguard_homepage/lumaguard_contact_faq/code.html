Technical & Care FAQ

How long is the LUMAGUARD warranty? expand_more
We provide a comprehensive 10-year limited warranty on all professional installations. This covers bubbling, peeling, delamination, and adhesive failure. Each installation comes with a digital certificate of authenticity.
What is the maintenance protocol for window films? expand_more
Wait at least 30 days after installation before the first cleaning to allow the adhesive to fully cure. Use a soft microfiber cloth and a mild soap-and-water solution. Avoid abrasive cleaners or ammonia-based products which can degrade the protective hard-coat.
How does ceramic film differ from standard dye? expand_more
Unlike dyed films that absorb heat and eventually fade, ceramic films utilize non-conductive, non-metallic nano-ceramic particles. They provide superior heat rejection (IR), 99.9% UV protection, and won't interfere with cellular or GPS signals.
Can films be applied to any glass type? expand_more
Most modern architectural glass is suitable for film application. However, we perform a thermal stress analysis for high-altitude or low-E glass to ensure compatibility and prevent thermal breakage.
